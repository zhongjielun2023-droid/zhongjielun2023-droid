## Selected Research

### FocusFlow: Reliable Local Control for Training-Free Video Editing

**First Author | AAAI 2027 Under Review**

FocusFlow introduces explicit local control for training-free video editing through Target Guidance Reallocation and Expectation-Confidence Filtering. It ranks first across all five evaluated metrics on FiVE-Bench Edit1-6 while improving non-target preservation and robustness to perturbed spatial priors.

### FlowTrack: Controlling Edit-Signal Execution in Inversion-Free Flow Video Editing

**First Author | NeurIPS 2026 Under Review**

FlowTrack decouples edit-signal estimation from execution and introduces a causal execution controller with a unique closed-form update. It achieves the best result on six of seven metrics over 419 evaluation samples.
